Four-bar rally-car linkage

A SolidWorks assembly of a rally car riding over a dune: the wheel is the crank, the car body is the coupler and a shock absorber is the rocker. Then a full two-sheet engineering drawing.

CourseME 357, Boston University
DateOctober 2026
ToolsSolidWorks

The assembly

LinkPartRole
GroundBase link, “desert dune”Fixed frame
CrankRally wheelInput, rotates
CouplerRally car bodyRides over the dune
RockerShock absorberSwings to follow the car

The links are joined with nylon spacers I specified in three lengths, plus aluminium 8-32 binding screws and barrels chosen from McMaster-Carr. I also animated the mechanism in a motion study.

The drawing

Dimensions are in inches, with tolerances of ±0.03 (one decimal place), ±0.015 (two) and ±0.005 (three), and ±1° on angles.
