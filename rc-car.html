RC car for Terrier Motorsport onboarding

Every new mechanical member of BU’s Formula SAE team designs and builds a small battery-powered car from shop stock. Mine had to be light, fast and quick to make.

The brief

Design and build an RC car using stock and tools from BU’s EPIC workshop, inside a 120 × 120 mm chassis. At least one part had to be laser-cut, one milled, one 3D-printed and one turned. The hardest part was fitting all of it into two weeks of manufacturing.

Design

The final design has a hanging aero cover, a clamp-fitted motor, an elevated battery mount, sprocket drive and 30 mm wheels. After the critical design review I made three changes:

  1. Raised the aero mount so the battery mount fits underneath.
  2. Redesigned the motor mount.
  3. Lowered the motor mount to bring the motor closer to the wheels.

What I learned

  • How to go from CAD to CAM to a finished part.
  • How to plan time across weeks rather than days.
  • Which oversights are easy to make, and how to catch them early.
  • How to use the manual mill, manual lathe, laser cutter and CNC mill.
